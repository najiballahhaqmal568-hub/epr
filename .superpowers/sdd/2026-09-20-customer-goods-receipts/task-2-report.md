## Implemented and interfaces

- `src/lib/sync.ts` encodes receipt source customer, onward buyer, and warehouse variant references by UUID and removes the sender's numeric IDs. Decoding requires the UUID master before any row write/effect; no foreign numeric fallback. Active documents require live masters. Cancelled audit may use an existing deleted master; a missing master remains a recoverable error. Receipt cash child `refId` is removed because it is local-only.
- `src/lib/customerGoodsReceiptSync.ts` exports `receiptReplayDecision(table, existing, incoming)`. A cancelled member cannot regress to a delayed active predecessor. Competing cancelled/correction metadata and stripped receipt markers leave evidence under `goodsReceiptConflict:<receiptUuid>:<table>:<rowUuid>` in `db.syncState`. `loadCustomerGoodsReceipt` consumes this key prefix and returns `status: 'conflict'` with a write-block reason. Missing anchor/children remain incomplete through the existing validator; sync does not pretend the rows are atomic.
- `applyRemoteRow` now reverse/applies receipt adjustment replacement, including tombstones, and rebuilds acquisition cost. Cancellation uses the manifest's saved `priorUnitCost` to restore the fallback basis before rebuilding. Non-receipt adjustment behavior was not changed.
- `src/lib/customerGoodsReceiptBackup.ts` exports `validateCustomerGoodsReceiptBackup(data)`. `importBackup` calls it before pausing sync or clearing tables. It reuses `validateCustomerGoodsReceiptRows`, then checks exact local ID↔UUID master mapping, liveness policy, aggregate member existence/reciprocity/amounts, correction predecessor/successor links, uniqueness and cycles. Orphan child or stripped marker is rejected. Cancelled audit tombstones remain in export/import.
- `goodsReceiptCompatibilityAcknowledged` is excluded from both backup export and import. It remains a separate per-device choice. `docs/sync-safety-review.md` records the sync behavior and operational limits.

## Limits and next safe step

There is no backend schema migration, server-side active-client version fence, or atomic multi-device aggregate transaction. A client seeing only a server's final same-row winner cannot reconstruct a sibling revision it never observed. Refresh all active clients before use, keep per-device acknowledgement separate, and edit a receipt on one device at a time after syncing. Do not use older clients once receipt records exist. Task 3 owns the UI and the deferred correction-preview cost-basis message. The two legacy local regressions were rerun successfully above; a full `npm test` run remains a pre-publish consideration.
